2) bin/quality-check.sh exit 143 at default job cap — MECHANISM FOUND, NO CODE CHANGE
   Scheduler facts from the script:
   - quality_check_default_max_background_jobs() returns detected_cpus/2
     hard-capped at 8 (and 2 on <=4-core machines).
   - There are 35 subgate labels; run_bg blocks in wait_for_bg_slot() until
     jobs -pr count (ticker excluded) drops below MAX_BACKGROUND_JOBS.
   - gateway_pest and cli_pest are long-running (minutes) and each holds one
     slot for its entire duration; the phase barrier at
     wait_for_bg_labels APP_BEFORE_PACKAGE_CHECK_LABELS additionally delays
     the 9 package subgates until every app static check has finished.
   - trap 'exit 143' TERM (line 56) converts an externally delivered SIGTERM
     into the observed exit 143. Nothing inside the script sends TERM to
     itself in the normal path.
   Mechanism: with a low cap (default 8 or less), the long Pest lanes plus the
   phase barrier serialize the ~33 short static checks behind slot waits, so
   total wall time balloons well past the invoking harness watchdog (e.g. the
   agent Bash tool 10-minute ceiling or a Solo worker timeout). The watchdog
   SIGTERMs the process group and the TERM trap surfaces exit 143. Setting
   ORBIT_QUALITY_CHECK_MAX_BACKGROUND_JOBS=40 (> 35 labels) removes throttling
   entirely, the run finishes inside the watchdog window, and the "failure"
   disappears. There is no wait-logic deadlock: running_bg_jobs uses jobs -pr,
   correctly excludes the progress ticker, and slots free as jobs exit.
   Why no fix applied: the death is caused by an external timeout policy, not
   by an internal scheduler bug, and the correct cap value is
   environment/policy-dependent (raising the ceiling from 8 to any specific
   number is a guess about the caller's watchdog and machine). Per lane
   instructions, changed nothing.
   Recommended follow-up (for the orchestrator/user to decide):
   a) Raise or remove the /2-with-ceiling-8 default on high-core machines
      (e.g. ceiling 16, or detected_cpus), since most subgates are short
      lint/format checks and gateway_pest --parallel already self-limits.
   b) Alternatively, exempt the long Pest lanes (gateway_pest, cli_pest) from
      the slot count so short static checks are never starved behind them.
   c) At minimum, document ORBIT_QUALITY_CHECK_MAX_BACKGROUND_JOBS as the
      knob for loop harnesses that run quality-check under a hard timeout.
