## Primitive

None. The renderer prints one concise success or failure line.

## Progress Tree

No progress tree. The command writes one gateway setup-step change and does not
run remote work.

## Human Strings

- Success: `App setup step '{title}' removed.`
- Failure: `app.setup_step_not_found: App setup step was not found.`
