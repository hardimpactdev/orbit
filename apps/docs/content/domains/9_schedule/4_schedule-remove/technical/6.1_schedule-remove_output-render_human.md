# Human Renderer: `orbit schedule:remove`

[Back to `schedule:remove` technical contract.](1_schedule-remove.md)

**Renderer:** Human terminal renderer.

## Primitive

- Progress: [Progress tree](../../../../ux/commands/progress/progress-tree.md)

## Selection

Use this renderer when `--json` is not present.

## Progress Tree

Render an in-progress tree after input resolution and destructive consent, and before gateway removal is written.

```text
┌ Removing Schedule
○ Resolve schedule
○ Apply and verify removal
○ Notify Orbit Scheduler
└ Working...
```

## Success Output

Render the schedule name, scope, target, and Orbit Scheduler pickup result.

## Failure Output

Validation and not-found failures render the rejected field or schedule name. Destructive-consent failures render no progress tree and state that no schedule was removed. Scheduler-unreachable failures render the schedule status, target node, and next doctor command: `doctor --family=schedule`.
