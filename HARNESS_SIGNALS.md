# Historical Harness Signals

This is historical evidence from the retired signal-ledger workflow. Do not add
new records during ordinary feature delivery and do not treat this file as an
active completion gate.

Existing records under `harness-signals/` remain readable for archaeology and
explicit human-requested loop diagnosis. Their claims describe the harness at
the time they were written; current workflow authority is `HARNESS.md`.

New reusable feedback belongs in `.orbit/feedback.jsonl` and closes through a
deterministic product protection or user-volunteered waiver. Qualifying process
failures use the trigger-only `loop-review` experiment contract.

`bin/orbit-harness-signal-index` remains a read-only historical indexing tool.
It is intentionally not part of `composer docs-lint` or feature finalization.
