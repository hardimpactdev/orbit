---
title: "orbit schedule:show"
sidebarTitle: "schedule:show"
description: "Show one Schedule with its command, `desired_timer_state`, `last_run_at`, and `last_run_status`, or one definition by name."
---

```bash
orbit schedule:show <schedule> [--project=ID] [--json]
```

| Argument | Required | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `schedule` | yes | Schedule UUID, or the definition name with `--project`. |
