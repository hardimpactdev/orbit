Question to Claude process 943:
How can Stage 3 deterministically prove failure of the second same-directory
rename without a public CLI/environment test flag, class abstraction, unsafe
permission trick, or nondeterministic obstruction race?

Advice and final adjudication:
Accept a private procedural replaceStagedCaptureDirectory(temp, final, backup,
?callable rename) helper. The callable replaces only the (from, to): bool rename
syscall and defaults inside the helper to native rename. Reach it in tests via
ORBIT_AGENT_SESSION_CAPTURE_TEST_NO_MAIN, a PHP require-time constant checked by
a final-lines main guard. The constant is not configurable through CLI or env,
so operator behavior remains unchanged. Keep exactly one production call site
and pass no callable there.

Required deterministic proof:
1. final-to-backup failure leaves final untouched and temp retained/reported.
2. temp-to-final failure restores backup, cleans temp, and fails loudly.
3. rollback failure names all paths, deletes nothing, and leaves backup/temp.
4. native same-filesystem success installs temp and removes backup afterward.
Also reject non-direct-child transaction paths before rename or recursive delete.
