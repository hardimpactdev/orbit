## Required CI proposal

Start with an informational `Gateway suite` job on a provisioned CI runner. Run the configured Unit/Feature suites on every PR and main push, alongside builds/static checks, with stock Pest parallel CPU detection and no TIA or changed-files filter. Make its stable job name part of the required aggregate only after the current failures and timing problems are repaired and a full CI job demonstrates an acceptable release cost. Keep privileged infrastructure coverage in its own correctly provisioned job.

The cleaned full audits took 206.884, 226.131 and 324.099 seconds locally with four workers; the latter two cover the incorporated main through df6f84a37. This variability and the remaining failures do not establish a fast, reliable required gate. Checkout/install and GitHub runner time have not been measured. Record total informational job time and slowest cases, then decide whether one job or stock shard support fits the release budget. Do not require a known-red job or hide failures behind an allowlist.

Prerequisites: PHP 8.5 and the existing PHP extensions, Gateway/SDK Composer dependencies, Git/OpenSSL/SSH tools used by the configured tests, Deno, and an authenticated checkout of a reviewed orbit-swamp commit supplied through ORBIT_SWAMP_CHECKOUT. Supply DENO_BIN when Deno is not on PATH. Do not use a moving sibling checkout. CI retains the approved five-second per-test budget; no suite/step time cap is proposed. Local validation retains two seconds and at most four workers.

This PR proposes the gate; it does not change CI jobs or branch protection. The migration-era build/static Required checks remain unchanged.
