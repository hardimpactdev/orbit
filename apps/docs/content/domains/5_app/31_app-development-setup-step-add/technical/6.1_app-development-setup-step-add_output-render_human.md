# Human output: `app-development-setup-step:add`

Success reports the new step ID, order, command, and timeout. Failure reports
the shared Orbit error message and exits non-zero.

## Progress Tree

No streamed progress is emitted.

## Primitive

None. The renderer prints two concise success lines or one failure message.
